A result reports only whether the message travelled here unmodified and unreplayed (§14.1). It says nothing about whether the message is wanted, or whether any declared modification was benign.

What this verifier checks

Checks follow draft-ietf-dkim-dkim2-spec-09. New in -09: a message whose history includes a null body Recipe ("b": null) is a PERMERROR (has a null recipe), since its earlier body cannot be checked; a message with no DKIM2 header fields at all is NONE. A From: is Authentic when its domain is at or below d=, as before, and now also when d= is below the From: domain, unless that domain's Domain Policy Record (a v=DKIM1 TXT record at _domainkey.domain) carries f=strict: then it forbids subdomain signing. A From: with more than one address, or at a domain literal, says so. A key record sitting next to another TXT record (such as v=spf1) is found; an RSA key under 1024 bits is too short; the first signature (i=1) may not carry the exploded flag. Still checked as in -08: s= and h= may offer alternatives (a,b|c passes if both a and b pass, or c does), and an algorithm this verifier does not implement (including ML-DSA-44, mldsa44) is an error unless an alternative passes; tag names and Recipe JSON keys must be lowercase; an invalid Recipe step is named (recipe b[1] is invalid); the Chain of Custody is checked hop by hop, and Recipes carry lines that are not UTF-8 as "u" steps. A pasted message has no delivery time, so timestamps are checked against now: a signature more than 14 days old shows as signature expired. When a message has more than one fault, the first error listed is the primary one and sets the result: the Message-Instances (structure, Recipes, then the hashes, top down) are checked before the signatures, and no public key is used until they all pass.

Public keys are fetched over DNS-over-HTTPS from Cloudflare (cloudflare-dns.com), so the signing domains and selectors in your message are sent to Cloudflare, and so is the From: domain when its Domain Policy Record has to be looked up. The message body never leaves your browser.
